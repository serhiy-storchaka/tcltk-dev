Side findings without a ticket

  • ico: getIcon -type ICODATA always fails (it runs file exists on the data), and writeIconICODATA is broken.
  • widgetv: widget::validator keeps references to destroyed entries.
  • widget::calendar: the Greek and Hebrew weekday names are double-encoded UTF-8; dateentry's initial date is UTC midnight, so it shows yesterday east of UTC just after midnight.
  • plotchart: vertical gradient backgrounds are not drawn (negative step), and methodProc(xyplot,bindcmd) refers to a proc with another signature.
  • 1346295fff: 23 plotchart files and files in other modules still have the executable bit in the repository.
